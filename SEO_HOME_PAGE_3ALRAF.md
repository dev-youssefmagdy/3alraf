## Title suffix

| Language | Text |
|---|---|
| AR | `عالرف` |
| EN | `3alraf` |

The suffix is added to every other page title, e.g. `Product name | 3alraf`. The home title already contains the brand, so it is not repeated there.

## Notes

- Brand and main search terms (gifts, beauty, accessories, Egypt) come first so Google is less likely to cut them off.
- Cash on delivery and fast shipping are in the descriptions because they help people decide to click.
- The Arabic description addresses women (`تسوّقي`), which suits beauty and gifts. For a neutral tone use `تسوّق` or `تسوقوا`.
- If the shop also sells home items, stationery or candles, add them to the title and description.
- The positioning is inferred from the catalogue and logo; adjust it if it is not right.
